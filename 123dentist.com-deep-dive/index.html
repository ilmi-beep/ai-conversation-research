What to fix first

The biggest problems, in the order an AI tool meets them. A tool that cannot reach or read your pages never gets as far as trusting them.

5 pages describe the business or a job title as a person

Failed check: Each Person in your structured data holds one person's name only

■ Medium Gate 2: read Error

Why it matters. A Person named after the business, or with a job title inside the name, makes machines think there is a person by that name. Facts about your business and your people get mixed up.

Fix. Use Organization for the business. For people, put only the name in name and the job title in jobTitle.

Breadcrumb markup on 1 page has fewer than two steps

Failed check: Each breadcrumb in your structured data has at least two steps

■ Medium Gate 2: read Error

Why it matters. A breadcrumb (the trail such as Home > Products > Scanners) needs at least two steps before Google uses it. A one-step trail says nothing about where the page sits.

Fix. Write the full trail from the home page to the current page into the BreadcrumbList.

Your structured data links to 1 ID that no page defines

Failed check: Every link inside your structured data points to something that is defined on your site

▲ High Gate 2: read Error

Why it matters. An @id (an ID inside the code) lets one block say, for example, "this article's publisher is our business". If the ID it points to is not defined anywhere, the link leads nowhere, so machines cannot tell who published the page.

Fix. Point these links at an ID that is defined, usually the business's main ID on the home page, or define the missing ID once.

What we found, and how you know it is done
  • Solution and industry pages: 1 of 6

Examples

  • https://www.123dentist.com/general-dentistry/: isRelatedTo points to https://www.123dentist.com/emergency-dentistry/#service, which is not defined anywhere on the site (on 1 page).

Done when: Every ID used in your structured data is defined on at least one page of your site.

Effort: A day or less

1 page is marked up as the wrong kind of page

Failed check: Each page is marked up as the kind of thing it shows: an article as an article, a product as a product, an event as an event

● Low Gate 2: read Error

Why it matters. The type in the structured data tells search engines and AI what the page is. An article marked up as a whole blog, or a product page with no product, is filed under the wrong kind of thing and misses the features for its real kind.

Fix. In each page template, use the type that fits the page: BlogPosting or Article for a single article or case study, Product for a product, Event for an event. Use Blog only on the blog's index page.

What we found, and how you know it is done
  • Articles and case studies: 1 of 239

Done when: Every article page has an article type (for example BlogPosting), every product page a Product, every event page an Event, and Blog appears only on the blog's index page.

Your structured data leaves out much of what your pages talk about

Failed check: Your structured data declares the business, products and services your pages talk about

▲ High Gate 3: quote Error

Why it matters. AI tools quote facts they can tie to a clear thing: this product, made by this business. If your pages mention products and services that the code never declares, AI has to guess what they are and who offers them.

Fix. Declare each product and service a page is about (Product, Service) and link it to the business (brand, provider or offers).

Your phone number is missing the Canadian country code

Failed check: Every phone number in your structured data starts with + and a valid country code

● Low Gate 4: trust Error

Why it matters. A phone number in structured data should be in international format: Google asks for the country code and the area code.

Fix. Change "6045539007" to "+1-604-553-9007" in the Organization.
